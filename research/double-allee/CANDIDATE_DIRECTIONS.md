# Double Allee — Candidate Directions

Each candidate must contain:

- precise mathematical question;
- closest known literature;
- novelty evidence;
- expected theorem program;
- likely obstruction/counterexample;
- relation to fractional stability;
- relation to networks/structured matrices if natural;
- data opportunity;
- reason it is more than a model variation;
- kill criterion.

Do not select a primary direction until competing candidates have been actively challenged.
