# Double Allee Research Branch

This branch is a **preferred opportunity lens** inside the broader project:

> **Dynamical Systems and Stability Theory — Fractional Differential Equations**

Its purpose is to map the mathematical state of knowledge around Double/Multiple Allee dynamics and identify research gaps that may be fertile for a **future** Q1-level project.

The branch does **not** itself develop the selected gap into a finished theorem paper.

## Preference rule

The eventual opportunity portfolio should prefer:

1. a gap directly involving a **Double Allee / Multiple Allee model**; or
2. if direct Double Allee problems are too shallow/crowded, a gap **very close** to them in:
   - threshold geometry;
   - persistence/extinction;
   - bifurcation/multistability;
   - robustness;
   - dispersal/network coupling;
   - fractional memory/order structure.

The ecological connection must be mathematically natural.

## Candidate policy

A candidate that survives an adversarial novelty search is retained in the opportunity portfolio.

It is **not** automatically promoted to:
- theorem proving;
- computational development;
- original-paper drafting.

Those belong to a separate future project after the state-of-the-art map is mature.

## Current lesson

The most developed candidate so far—mechanism-space / persistence-filtered composition—survived only in narrowed form. Most of its individual mathematical ingredients are standard in adjacent literatures.

This is precisely why the broader FDE domain map must be completed before any final research recommendation.
