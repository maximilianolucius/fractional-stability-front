# Double Allee — Novelty Audit

For each surviving candidate direction:

1. search exact terminology;
2. search synonyms and equivalent formulations;
3. search the mathematical theorem without ecological terminology;
4. inspect adjacent literatures;
5. backward citation chase;
6. forward citation chase;
7. inspect recent preprints;
8. inspect follow-up work by key authors;
9. compare against major reviews;
10. document unresolved ambiguity.

Use dated, reproducible search evidence.

Before exhaustive verification, say only:

> No result resolving X was identified in the searched corpus as of YYYY-MM-DD.
