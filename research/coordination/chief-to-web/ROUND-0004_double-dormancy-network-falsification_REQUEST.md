## Objective

Aggressively test whether the proposed **double-dormancy / emergent-threshold** research line is genuinely underdeveloped mathematically, especially when extended to networked populations.

The core phenomenon is:

> two component Allee mechanisms that individually do not generate a strong demographic threshold can jointly create one.

The search must not depend on the phrase “Double Allee.”

## Exact questions

1. Has a general theorem already characterized when two or more component fitness mechanisms jointly create a strong demographic Allee threshold?
2. Are there necessary-and-sufficient conditions for:
   - threshold emergence;
   - uniqueness versus multiplicity of positive thresholds;
   - monotone movement of threshold location with component strength;
   - fold/saddle-node creation of the threshold?
3. Is Berec et al.'s “double dormancy” concept developed mathematically beyond illustrative models?
4. Do general scalar population-growth, catastrophe, fold, monotone-comparative-statics, survival/fecundity, or nonlinear composition literatures already subsume the intended theorem?
5. For coupled patches/networks, do existing theorems characterize how dispersal/topology transforms a local emergent threshold?
6. Search deterministic and stochastic frameworks:
   - graph Laplacian coupling;
   - cooperative/monotone systems;
   - bistable networks;
   - metapopulations;
   - reaction-diffusion on graphs;
   - interacting particle systems;
   - coupled saddle-node systems;
   - rescue effects;
   - source-sink systems.
7. Are there arbitrary-graph or topology-parameter theorems that make C-DA-2 already covered?
8. What minimal assumptions would be required for a nontrivial general theorem if the literature does not already contain one?

## Working hypotheses to challenge

H1:
> a reusable composition theorem for double dormancy may be missing.

H2:
> a general network theorem for thresholds generated by interacting component effects may be missing even though strong-Allee network theory exists.

Search specifically for results that invalidate either hypothesis.

## Scope

Ecology, mathematical biology, nonlinear dynamics, bifurcation theory, monotone systems, network dynamics, metapopulations, interacting particle systems, and applied probability.

Include papers without the words Allee/Double Allee when the mathematics is equivalent.

## Exclusions

- model-specific predator–prey papers unless they prove a reusable theorem;
- papers that only numerically display a threshold;
- simple fractionalization;
- continuum diffusion alone unless it yields a general theorem transferable to graphs/networks.

Backward/forward citation chase Berec et al. 2007 and Lanchier 2013. Search authors who cite “double dormancy.”

## Evidence required

For each potentially resolving theorem:

- function/model class;
- whether mechanisms are explicitly separable into components;
- exact threshold definition;
- number of equilibria/unstable thresholds;
- necessity/sufficiency;
- bifurcation mechanism;
- network assumptions;
- topology dependence;
- whether result is arbitrary graph or special graph;
- whether stochastic/deterministic;
- exact relation to C-DA-1 or C-DA-2.
