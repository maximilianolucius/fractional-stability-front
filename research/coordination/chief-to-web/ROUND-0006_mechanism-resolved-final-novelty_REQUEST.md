## Exact candidate class

The demographic sign is controlled by

V_S(x) = B(x) product_{i in S} A_i(x),

with positive component factors A_i and threshold equation

V_S(x)=1.

Component deletion is

A_i -> 1.

Double dormancy means:
- each one-component deletion system is non-strong;
- the joint system has a strong positive demographic threshold.

The proposed shape-controlled theorem uses a strictly unimodal V_S, often certified through the log-slope

L_S = B'/B + sum_i A_i'/A_i.

The inverse problem observes aggregate x(t) while the hidden mechanisms enter through

P(x)=product_i A_i(x).

The proposed invariance is

A_i -> A_i h_i, with product_i h_i=1.
