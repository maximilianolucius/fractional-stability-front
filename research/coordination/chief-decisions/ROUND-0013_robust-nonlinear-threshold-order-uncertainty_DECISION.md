## 1. Executive decision

ROUND-0013 falsifies the broad claim that robust fractional dynamics is poorly developed.

The following are already mature or substantially developed:

- robust linear fractional stability;
- simultaneous coefficient/order uncertainty;
- incommensurate robust linear stability;
- nonlinear class-specific robust equilibrium stability;
- fractional viability and memo-viability;
- classical robust permanence;
- classical robust regions of attraction;
- multiparameter fractional stability/bifurcation regions.

The residual opportunity is much narrower:

> **uncertainty-uniform global survival/extinction geometry and basin-relative persistence for positive nonlinear Caputo systems under simultaneous parameter and fractional-order uncertainty.**

No resolving theorem for that exact object was identified in the searched corpus.

## 2. What is closed as novelty

The following are not viable novelty claims:

- uncertainty in (alpha) itself;
- simultaneous parameter and order uncertainty;
- robust incommensurate linear stability;
- nonlinear robust Mittag-Leffler stability in general;
- fractional viability;
- common robust ROA as a mathematical idea;
- multiparameter fractional stability regions.

All have direct or close prior art.

## 4. Why Double Allee is a natural realization

Strong/Double Allee dynamics forces the theory to confront:

- multiple attractors;
- extinction basins containing positive states;
- thresholds/separatrices;
- parameter-dependent folds/cusps;
- order-dependent stability and memory effects.

Therefore it is a much stronger testbed than a one-equilibrium robust-stability problem.

The search did not establish that these transfer essentially verbatim to simultaneous parameter/order uncertainty in positive Caputo multistable systems.
