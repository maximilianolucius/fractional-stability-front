## Adversarial assessment

The search did what it was supposed to do: it eliminated the broad network claim and forced the surviving composition idea into a mathematically precise form.

C-DA-1 survives only as a **general mechanism-resolved composition theorem**. It cannot claim that two component effects or emergent thresholds are themselves new.

C-DA-2, as originally phrased, is not sufficiently novel. Arbitrary-network persistence/extinction theory already handles heterogeneous logistic/bistable local growth, and recent graph-Allee literature goes further into equilibrium-count/bifurcation structure. A network extension remains useful only if it preserves the internal component decomposition in a way generic bistable-node theorems cannot express.

## Evidence accepted

### Against broad novelty

- Lan 2025 directly studies a stochastic single-species model with two component Allee effects and rigorous deterministic/stochastic threshold results.
- Berec et al. 2007 already defines double dormancy and analyzes a concrete two-component mechanism model.
- de Roos–Persson–Thieme and van Kooten–de Roos–Persson establish emergent Allee/bistability mechanisms in structured ecological systems.
- Stehlík–Švígler–Volek 2023 gives persistence/extinction conditions on arbitrary connected undirected graphs with heterogeneous local growth including bistable reactions.
- additional graph/metapopulation Allee prior art makes “topology changes the threshold” non-novel.

### Residual gap

No searched source provides the exact intended theorem over a **separable component-function class** with all of:

1. component deletion/counterfactual definition;
2. each component individually non-strong;
3. necessary-and-sufficient joint strong-threshold creation;
4. uniqueness/multiplicity control;
5. threshold comparative statics;
6. fold/nondegeneracy characterization.

## C-DA-1 disposition

**SURVIVES — PROMOTE TO LEADING CANDIDATE.**

But it must be formulated without relying on the phrase “Double Allee.”

The mathematical object is:

> composition of separately interpretable density-dependent fitness components and the emergence of a demographic threshold that is absent under specified component-deletion counterfactuals.

## C-DA-2 disposition

**REJECT AS STANDALONE PRIMARY DIRECTION.**

Retain only as a later extension:
