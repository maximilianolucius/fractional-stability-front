## Executive finding

The scalar route is closed, but a genuinely fractional multidimensional/history-state frontier survives.

### Scalar killer

For a one-dimensional autonomous Caputo equation with (0<alpha<1), distinct solution trajectories do not intersect under the standard separation hypotheses.

Since (x(t)equiv a) is the equilibrium trajectory at the strong-Allee threshold,

[
x_0<aRightarrow x(t)<a,qquad
x_0>aRightarrow x(t)>a.
]

Hence the scalar equilibrium Allee threshold is an **order-independent basin barrier**. Caputo memory can alter transients but cannot shift or cross this equilibrium separator in the canonical scalar IVP.

### Multidimensional survival

In dimensions (d>1):
- physical trajectories may meet;
- (x(t)) alone is not the full Markov state;
- the natural Caputo semidynamical system lives in a function/memory space;
- published ecological basin plots generally vary (x(0)) only and therefore show the basin restricted to a physical-initial-data embedding.

Direct Double-Allee prior art already computes basin stability numerically and includes incommensurate memory orders, but no searched paper gives a rigorous (alpha)-dependent survival/extinction separatrix in the full memory-state semiflow.

The remaining opportunity is therefore **not “fractional memory shifts the Allee threshold”**. It is:

> characterize multidimensional survival/extinction basin geometry in the Caputo memory state, its physical-initial-data slice, and its relation to basin-relative persistence.

# 10. Numerical-artifact / initialization warning

### Killer 4

**PARTLY FIRES.**

Fractional basin plots require special care because:
- convergence can be algebraically slow;
- finite-time classification may misidentify long transients;
- physical-initial-data plots sample only one finite-dimensional family inside the memory state;
- basin-volume probabilities depend on sampling measure;
- solver horizon/tolerance/memory discretization matter.

Leng–Lin–Kurths 2016, DOI **10.1038/srep21449**, provides the analogous warning for delayed infinite-dimensional basins.

Thus direct numerical evidence of (alpha)-dependent basin fractions is real evidence, but not by itself a theorem about the full invariant memory-state basin.

# 11. Connection to threshold-relative persistence

**CREDIBLE AND NONTRIVIAL.**

This is the mathematically real D10-D/D11 intersection.
