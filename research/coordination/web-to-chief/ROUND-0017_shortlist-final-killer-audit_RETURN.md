# FAMILY A — DETERMINISTIC REACHABLE MEMORY-STATE BASIN GEOMETRY

## A verdict

# **A-SURVIVES-GENUINE**

### Exact residual

For an autonomous positive multidimensional Caputo system with at least two asymptotic outcomes, characterize the fibers

\[
\mathcal F_x
=
e_0^{-1}(x)\cap\mathcal R_\alpha
\]

of the present-state projection restricted to the physically reachable memory-state set, and determine whether

\[
\mathcal F_x\cap\mathcal B(A_1)\neq\varnothing,
\qquad
\mathcal F_x\cap\mathcal B(A_2)\neq\varnothing,
\qquad
A_1\neq A_2.
\]

The strongest preferred realization is extinction versus survival/coexistence in a strong/Double-Allee positive system.

The novelty is **not**:
- existence of a history state;
- same-present/different-memory;
- history-dependent tipping;
- multistability itself.

Those are prior.

The residual is the **fiberwise organization of asymptotic basins on the physically reachable Caputo state space**.

---

## A1. Stable-set fiber / hereditary killer

A deep search was run through:

- hereditary systems;
- Volterra equations;
- infinite-delay equations;
- stable sets;
- factor maps;
- noninjective observables;
- partial observation;
- output equivalence;
- same output / different omega-limits.

### Strongest adjacent prior

Huang–Yang–Yi–Zou (2014), DOI 10.1016/j.jde.2013.12.015, studies a bistable delay differential equation with:
- two asymptotically stable equilibria;
- basin-of-attraction subsets in the full history phase space;
- heteroclinic structure;
- a population application with Allee effect.

This is important because it confirms that in hereditary dynamics basin membership is naturally a **history-state** property.

It does **not** prove that two histories with the same current endpoint must belong to the same basin.

No theorem was located with the implication

\[
\pi(z_1)=\pi(z_2)
\Longrightarrow
\omega(z_1)=\omega(z_2)
\]

for the endpoint projection of a general hereditary/Volterra system.

Nor was a theorem located that forbids one endpoint fiber from meeting two stable basins in the physically reachable Caputo set.

### Killer outcome

**DOES NOT FIRE.**

The adjacent literature supports the relevance of history-space basins but does not collapse them to current-state basins.

---

## A2. Positive / monotone / competitive killer

### Direct comparison prior

Wu (2023), DOI 10.1016/j.chaos.2023.113437, gives general comparison principles for systems of Caputo fractional-order ODEs, including quasi-monotone, mixed quasi-monotone and mixed monotone systems.

Later 2026 comparison work strengthens this system-comparison framework.

Scalar and triangular Caputo systems remain especially restrictive:
- scalar trajectories do not intersect;
- triangular systems inherit strong nonintersection/ODE-like behavior.

### What the comparison theory actually kills

Family A should **exclude**:
- scalar systems;
- triangular systems;
- claims based only on two strictly ordered/comparable initial states when comparison theory forces their order forever.

### What it does not kill

No theorem was identified showing that in a natural multidimensional competitive or predator–prey Caputo system

\[
e_0^{-1}(x)\cap\mathcal R_\alpha
\]

must lie in a single basin for **all** reachable histories.

Comparison/order preservation controls comparable states. It does not establish injectivity of the present-state projection nor basin uniqueness for arbitrary incomparable reachable histories.

### Killer outcome

**PARTIAL ONLY.**

Monotonicity narrows the admissible model class but does not eliminate the candidate.

---

## A3. Concrete realizability

A strong direct ecological class exists.

Mondal–Pal–Takeuchi–Mukherjee–Kesh–Saha (2025), DOI 10.1016/j.cjph.2025.09.020:

> *Dynamics of a fractional order predator-prey system with double Allee effect and group defense.*

The paper has:
- a positive predator–prey system;
- strong/double Allee dynamics;
- stable extinction;
- coexistence and other alternative states;
- multistability;
- basin-of-attraction computations;
- incommensurate orders.

This paper does **not** establish fiberwise multibasin ambiguity.

But it shows that Family A does not require a pathological linear construction merely to obtain the ecological ingredients:
- positivity;
- multidimensionality;
- nontriangular predator–prey coupling;
- multiple asymptotic outcomes;
- direct Double-Allee structure.

### Realizability verdict

**NATURALLY REALIZABLE AS A THEOREM QUESTION.**

The remaining step is to determine whether such classes admit or forbid multibasin fibers.

---

## A4. Theorem-family richness

Family A is not forced to reduce to one counterexample.

A serious theorem family could contain:

1. **impossibility results**
   - scalar;
   - triangular;
   - selected monotone/comparable subclasses;

2. **existence results**
   - sufficient structural conditions for multibasin fibers;

3. **fiber geometry**
   - topology;
   - regularity;
   - multiplicity;
   - local/global structure;

4. **order dependence**
   - how fiberwise basin intersections depend on \(\alpha\);

5. **observable sufficiency**
   - conditions under which current population is or is not a sufficient asymptotic-state observable;

6. **positive ecological specialization**
   - extinction/coexistence in strong/Double-Allee systems.

This is theorem-family scale, not merely a numerical example.

# FAMILY B — ROBUST GLOBAL THRESHOLD GEOMETRY

## B verdict

# **B-REFORMULATE**

The broad Family B formulation is too close to mature robust persistence/viability theory.

A narrower memory-specific multibasin geometry remains defensible.

---

## B1. Robust permanence after a history-state lift

### Strongest killer prior

Smith–Zhao (2001), DOI 10.1016/S0362-546X(01)00678-2:

> *Robust persistence for semidynamical systems.*

This is already abstract semiflow theory, not merely finite-dimensional ODE theory.

Salceanu (2022), DOI 10.3934/dcdsb.2021258:

> *Robust uniform persistence for structured models of delay differential equations.*

This explicitly handles structured biological populations with delay/history and proves robust uniform persistence using:
- Lyapunov exponents;
- invariant probability measures;
- boundary repulsion.

Together with Schreiber / Hofbauer–Schreiber / Patel–Schreiber, this means:

> robust persistence after enlargement to an infinite-dimensional history state is not by itself a new theorem program.

### Perturbing the fractional index

Doan–Kloeden–Tuan, *Attractors of Caputo Fractional Differential Equations* (2025/2026), DOI 10.1007/978-3-032-05511-8, includes continuous dependence of Caputo solutions on the fractional index.

Recent stochastic work similarly gives Lipschitz dependence on the order in finite-time solution metrics.

Therefore:

> varying \(\alpha\) is not automatically a singular/discontinuous perturbation that defeats all classical robustness theory.

### Why the killer is still incomplete

Robust permanence theorems typically concern persistence of an interior set under perturbations of a semiflow/vector field.

Strong Allee dynamics is different:
- extinction is a legitimate stable outcome;
- global permanence on all positive states is false;
- the object is a **multibasin separator and basin-relative persistence** problem.

Moreover varying \(\alpha\):
- changes the power-law memory kernel;
- changes the lifted semigroup/operator;
- requires uniform asymptotic control, not only finite-horizon continuous dependence.

No source was found proving that standard robust-persistence perturbation hypotheses hold uniformly for an order interval while simultaneously preserving a strong-Allee basin separator.

### Killer outcome

**FIRES AGAINST BROAD ROBUST-PERSISTENCE NOVELTY, NOT AGAINST MULTIBASIN ROBUST GEOMETRY.**

---

## B2. Fractional differential inclusions / strong invariance

Direct fractional viability exists.

Carja et al. (2014), DOI 10.1016/j.aml.2014.06.012, gives viability results for Caputo fractional differential inclusions.

Memo-viability work further develops memory-aware tangency theory.

However the dominant direct results located are of the form:

> there exists a viable selection/solution.

Family B needs the stronger universal quantifier:

\[
\forall(\boldsymbol\alpha,\theta,m)\in\mathcal U,
\quad
\text{the required survival/extinction conclusion holds.}
\]

Ordinary differential-inclusion theory has mature **strong invariance**, discriminating kernels and maximal robust invariant sets, but no direct theorem was located supplying the full Caputo/order-uncertain strong-Allee threshold object.

### Killer outcome

**PARTIAL.**

The generic set-valued language is mature; the exact fractional multibasin object remains unclosed.

---

## B3. Exactness versus conservative certificates

This is the principal weakness of Family B.

A future project that only proves:

\[
V(x)\le c
\Longrightarrow
\text{survival for all uncertainties}
\]

through one common Lyapunov inequality risks being only another conservative robust certificate.

The mathematically stronger residual is:

- maximal or sharp common survival/extinction sets;
- separator enclosure with provable error/sharpness;
- conditions for preservation/loss of bistability;
- continuity/bifurcation of basin boundaries under \(\alpha\)- and parameter perturbations.

No general exact theory for these objects was located.

### Accessibility assessment

**PLAUSIBLE BUT HIGH-RISK.**

Exact maximal sets may be hard; the family should be framed around structural theorems, not merely computational certificates.

---

## B4. Mechanism structure

Multiple/Double-Allee mechanisms can still be valuable here, but as **structured uncertainty**, not as new abstract geometry.

Meaningful structures include:
- correlated parameter families;
- mechanism-preserving perturbations;
- component failures constrained by biological meaning;
- common low-density thresholds;
- uncertainty sets that preserve or destroy the strong-Allee topology.

This gives ecological substance without reviving C6 as a separate frontier.

---

## B exact reformulated residual

Family B should become:

> **Robust multibasin threshold geometry for positive Caputo systems under parameter and memory-kernel/order perturbations:** characterize when extinction/survival bistability and its basin separator persist, and obtain sharp/common inner or exact survival/extinction regions, with basin-relative persistence on the survival side.

This is narrower than “robust fractional permanence.”

# FAMILY C — STOCHASTIC MEMORY-STATE PERSISTENCE / RARE EXIT

## C verdict

# **C-REFORMULATE**

The abstract persistence and switching layers now have very strong prior.

The surviving core is **rare extinction / basin exit for singular fractional-memory systems**, with strong-Allee conditional persistence as context rather than the primary abstract novelty.

---

## C1. Földes–Stacy compatibility test

Földes–Stacy 2026, arXiv:2601.19145, works on a Polish state space \(M\) with:

- a closed invariant extinction set \(M_0\);
- invariant persistence region \(M_+=M\setminus M_0\);
- a Feller Markov process;
- dissipative Lyapunov/tightness conditions;
- an average Lyapunov function whose generator has negative average over all invariant measures supported on \(M_0\);
- martingale/quadratic-variation controls;
- an infinite-dimensional compactness/tightness hypothesis.

Their main persistence theorem gives:
- invariant measures in \(M_+\);
- asymptotic occupation of compact subsets of \(M_+\);
- stochastic persistence.

### Comparison with Hamaguchi/Bianchi lift

Hamaguchi 2024 supplies for completely monotone stochastic Volterra kernels:
- separable Hilbert memory state;
- lifted Markov process;
- Feller-type structure;
- existence/uniqueness.

Bianchi et al. 2026 strengthens:
- invariant measures;
- stationary processes;
- limit distributions;
- long-time laws.

Therefore several Földes–Stacy hypotheses are structurally compatible with the lifted SVE framework.

### What is not automatic

For an ecological fractional Volterra system one still must verify:

1. the extinction set is closed and invariant in the lifted state;
2. the positive region is invariant;
3. an admissible average Lyapunov observable has a well-defined generator on the lift;
4. boundary invariant measures can be classified or controlled;
5. the required tightness/compactness estimates hold;
6. the biological current-population observable interacts correctly with the memory coordinates.

### Transfer classification

**TECHNICALLY NONTRIVIAL BUT ABSTRACTLY STANDARD.**

This is not enough novelty for a wholly new stochastic-persistence theory.

The persistence part of C should be treated as:
- a specialization/extension of Földes–Stacy-type machinery;
- possibly nontrivial because of the singular Volterra lift and strong-Allee boundary geometry;
- but not the main novelty claim.

---

## C2. Boundary/extinction geometry on the lift

Stochastic Volterra positivity is direct prior:
- completely monotone kernels can preserve nonnegativity;
- general singular kernels admit convex-domain invariance under suitable coefficient structures.

Thus ecologically admissible positive noise is feasible.

For strong Allee, however, the relevant state decomposition is not simply:

\[
\partial \mathbb R_+^d
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\operatorname{int}\mathbb R_+^d.
\]

There may be:
- an interior extinction basin;
- a survival/coexistence basin;
- memory states with the same present population but different deterministic basin membership.

This makes **basin-relative stochastic persistence** different from standard coexistence/permanence away from the boundary.

No general stochastic-Volterra theorem was located that performs this strong-Allee basin-relative decomposition.

### Residual

Real, but best coupled to the rare-exit problem rather than sold as abstract persistence theory.

---

## C3. Quasipotential / exit-time killer

### Strong adjacent killer

Lipshutz (2018), DOI 10.3934/dcds.2018135:

> *Exit time asymptotics for small noise stochastic delay differential equations.*

This paper:
- proves a sample-path LDP uniform over bounded sets of initial histories;
- defines the quasipotential on the delay-history state space;
- derives asymptotic estimates for exit times from domains attracted to stable equilibria or periodic orbits.

Therefore:

> “history-state quasipotential and exit theory is unknown”

is false.

### Direct stochastic-Volterra prior

Jacquier–Pannier 2022 gives pathwise large deviations for broad stochastic Volterra systems.

Gao et al. 2026 gives a Freidlin–Wentzell LDP directly for weakly singular power-law stochastic Volterra equations.

### What was not found

No direct theorem was identified for singular power-law Caputo/Volterra memory deriving the full downstream package:

- quasipotential on the Markovian memory lift;
- uniform LDP over relevant lifted initial-memory sets;
- asymptotic exit-time law from deterministic fractional-memory basins;
- most likely extinction path;
- dependence on \(\alpha\);
- extinction/survival transition geometry in a strong/Double-Allee positive system.

### Killer outcome

**PARTIAL BUT SEVERE.**

The conceptual machinery is classical/adjacent.

The nontrivial residual is the singular fractional-memory implementation and its basin geometry.

---

## C4. Herrera-Marín scope test

### arXiv:2605.00729

This already studies nonlinear operator-valued Volterra dynamics with:
- completely monotone long-memory kernels;
- finite-state Markov switching;
- annealed stability;
- quenched almost-sure growth;
- intermittency/metastable bursts.

### arXiv:2606.23558

*Regime-Switching Volterra Operators: Modal Stability and Quenched Amplification.*

This follow-up further develops:
- regime-dependent Volterra operators;
- global well-posedness;
- continuity across switches;
- modal stability conditions;
- quenched amplification;
- commuting/noncommuting operator cases.

### Scope conclusion

Persistent long-memory regime switching is now **too directly covered** to serve as Family C's primary novelty.

Switching should remain:
- an application architecture;
- a stress test;
- possibly an ecological realization.

It should not be the core theorem claim.

---

## C5. Positivity / noise realism

Family C survives the ecological-admissibility test.

Direct stochastic Volterra invariance theory supports:
- nonnegative orthant preservation;
- multiplicative/square-root-type positive noise;
- small-noise scaling compatible with positivity under suitable coefficient structure.

Thus one need not rely on additive Gaussian noise that sends populations negative.

### Modeling requirement

Any future strong-Allee stochastic-memory project should explicitly specify a positive-cone-preserving noise class.

---

## C exact reformulated residual

Family C should become:

> **Rare extinction and basin-exit theory for positive multistable stochastic fractional-memory systems:** starting from existing stochastic-Volterra Markov lifts and path LDPs, derive quasipotential/action geometry and exit-time asymptotics for extinction/survival basins, quantify dependence on the memory exponent/kernel, and treat stochastic persistence/regime switching as supporting layers rather than the primary novelty.

This is narrower but mathematically cleaner.

# Negative-search limitations

The following are search-qualified statements, not absence proofs.

1. No hereditary theorem was located forcing endpoint/current-state fibers to remain inside one basin for general multidimensional positive Caputo systems.
2. No theorem was located proving robust strong-Allee separator geometry uniformly over an interval/box of fractional orders.
3. No direct singular power-law stochastic-Volterra quasipotential/exit theorem was located that closes the strong-Allee rare-extinction problem.
4. 2026 stochastic-Volterra literature is moving quickly; Family C has the highest novelty-decay risk.
5. Continuous dependence on \(\alpha\) means Family B cannot rely on “order perturbation is fundamentally singular” as its novelty argument.
