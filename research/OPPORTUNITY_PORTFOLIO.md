# Candidate admission rule

New opportunities should include:

- exact mathematical statement;
- strongest neighboring result;
- evidence class;
- current novelty confidence;
- FDE centrality;
- Double Allee proximity;
- likely depth/unification value;
- main killer threat;
- next evidence needed.

# Selection rule

Do not choose a single winner until the full domain map has adequate breadth.

The final deliverable may recommend several opportunities rather than one, if the evidence does not justify a unique choice.
