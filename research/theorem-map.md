## Relationship vocabulary

- strictly generalizes;
- equivalent under assumptions;
- special case of;
- strengthens conclusion;
- weakens assumptions;
- incomparable;
- apparent contradiction explained by assumptions;
- computational reformulation.

Never add an implication edge without checking assumptions on both endpoints.
