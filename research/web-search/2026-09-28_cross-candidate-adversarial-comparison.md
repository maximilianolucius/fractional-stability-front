## 2. C1 — DA-MEMORY-FIBER

### New hostile search family

- noninjective observable + multistability;
- partial observation + different attractors;
- factor maps + basin fibers;
- indistinguishable present states + different omega limits;
- memory equations + same output/different attractor.

### Search result

General observability/factor-map literatures discuss noninjective projections and indistinguishable states, but no theorem was located resolving the physically reachable Caputo fiber:

\[
e_0^{-1}(x)\cap\mathcal R_\alpha
\]

with two distinct asymptotic basins.

### Residual

The exact basin-fiber theorem survives.

### Comparative status

RETAIN.

## 3. C2 — ROBUST-THRESHOLD

### New hostile search family

- robust viability kernel;
- discriminating kernel;
- robust capture basin;
- robust invariant set under disturbances;
- uncertain semiflow permanence;
- bistable robust basin.

### New killer prior

Han–Gao 2017, DOI 10.1186/s13662-017-1429-2:
discriminating kernels via viability/reachability.

Peng–Stancu–Dang 2019, DOI 10.1002/rnc.4577:
robust capture basin and discriminating kernel for uncertain nonlinear systems, with guaranteed interval approximations.

### Implication

The set-valued geometry of “survive for all disturbances” is standard viability/robust-control mathematics.

The novelty burden must therefore sit in:
- fractional history state;
- order uncertainty;
- strong-Allee multistability;
- uncontrolled persistence/extinction;
- exact/maximal rather than generic robust-invariant terminology.

### Comparative status

RETAIN narrowly.

## 4. C3 — STOCH-PERSISTENCE-EXIT

### New hostile search family

- stochastic persistence infinite dimensions;
- stochastic functional differential persistence;
- boundary invariant measures on Hilbert-space processes;
- stochastic Volterra persistence;
- quasipotential for stochastic Volterra;
- metastability non-Markov memory.

### Major new threat

Földes–Stacy 2026, arXiv:2601.19145:

General stochastic persistence criteria in infinite dimensions using average Lyapunov functions and invariant measures; applications include stochastic functional differential equations and ecological SPDEs.

### Consequence

The statement

> “persistence on an infinite-dimensional stochastic memory state needs a new abstract theory”

is no longer safe.

### Surviving residue

A direct bridge is still missing between:
- singular stochastic-Volterra Markov lift;
- extinction boundary geometry;
- strong-Allee basin-relative persistence;
- rare exit/quasipotential;
- fractional-order dependence.

### Comparative status

RETAIN, but rare-exit/basin-relative layer carries more novelty than persistence theory alone.

## 5. C4 — PERSISTENT-SWITCHING

### New hostile search family

- random switching Volterra;
- Markov switching long-memory evolution;
- nonlocal regime switching;
- piecewise coefficients hereditary systems;
- no-reset memory switching.

### Major new threat

Herrera-Marín 2026, arXiv:2605.00729:

Randomly switching nonlinear operator-valued Volterra systems with completely monotone long-memory kernels and Markov-chain modulation.

The paper proves:
- averaged subcriticality / moment control;
- memory-adapted Lyapunov estimates;
- quenched almost-sure growth exponent;
- intermittent bursts;
- metastable long-memory regime-switching behavior.

### Consequence

Persistent long-memory stochastic switching is already a direct research object.

The Caputo fixed-lower-terminal + Allee persistence question is still not solved, but C4 is too close to C3 and too exposed to fast 2026 prior to remain independent.

### Comparative status

MERGE INTO C3.

## 6. C5 — NONHYPERBOLIC-MEMORY

### New hostile search family

- center manifold weakly singular Volterra;
- center manifold integral equations with infinite delay;
- incommensurate fractional normal form;
- Lyapunov–Schmidt fractional bifurcation;
- singular resolvent bifurcation.

### Major adjacent prior

Matsunaga–Murakami–Nagabuchi–Minh 2015, DOI 10.1619/fesi.58.87:
- center-manifold theorem for integral equations with infinite delay;
- local exponential attractivity;
- stability reduction to a finite-dimensional central equation.

Combined with:
- Diekmann–van Gils Volterra center manifolds;
- 2024 Caputo–Hadamard fold normal form;
- prior Caputo fold/transcritical/pitchfork normal-form literature,

the generic “fractional memory lacks nonhyperbolic reduction” claim is too weak.

### Residual

Singular Caputo/incommensurate-specific theory may still be absent, but killer risk is too high relative to C1/C2/C3.

### Comparative status

ELIMINATE standalone.

## 7. C6 — MECHANISM-PERSISTENCE-FILTER

### New hostile search family

- multiple component Allee mathematical interaction;
- threshold logic ecology;
- component-to-demographic Allee;
- two component Allee stochastic threshold;
- multiple Allee bifurcation.

### Direct prior

Berec–Angulo–Courchamp 2007, DOI 10.1016/j.tree.2006.12.002:
multiple component Allee effects, interaction patterns, double dormancy and threshold amplification.

Lan 2025, DOI 10.1016/j.chaos.2025.116952:
two component Allee effects with a sharp stochastic threshold:
- extinction a.s. below threshold;
- strong stochastic permanence above threshold.

Yang–Fan 2026, DOI 10.1002/mma.70728:
predator–prey with multiple Allee effects, bistability and codimension-rich bifurcation structure.

### Consequence

C6 remains biologically important but has weak FDE essentiality and substantial direct ecological prior.

Its strongest remaining value is as a structured-mechanism layer inside C2.

### Comparative status

MERGE INTO C2.

## 13. Conclusion

The adversarial comparison does not support retaining seven independent candidates.

It supports three mathematically distinct and ecologically relevant families:

1. deterministic memory-state basin geometry;
2. robust global threshold geometry;
3. stochastic memory-state persistence / rare exit.

No final ordering is asserted.
