# 2. Multiple vital-rate/component composition is established prior art

The searched life-history/demographic literature already decomposes population growth into density-dependent vital-rate components.

## Rodriguez (1988)

Diego J. Rodriguez, *Models of growth with density regulation in more than one life stage*, Theoretical Population Biology 34(2):93–117. DOI **10.1016/0040-5809(88)90036-6**.

The paper studies density dependence in both preadult survival and fecundity under several functional forms. With exponential density dependence in both stages, the model can have up to three equilibria, two stable.

**Threat:** multiple density-regulated demographic components generating alternative stable states is decades-old mathematics.

**Difference from frozen framework:** no component-deletion definition of dormancy and no general theorem over arbitrary positive factor functions.

## Feldman & Morris (2011)

*Higher survival at low density counteracts lower fecundity to obviate Allee effects in a perennial plant*. Journal of Ecology 99:1162–1170. DOI **10.1111/j.1365-2745.2011.01855.x**.

They parameterize a density-dependent projection matrix from separate fecundity, survival and growth measurements and explicitly turn density dependencies on/off. Positive density dependence in fecundity alone generates an Allee effect, while adding improved low-density survival removes it.

**Threat:** mechanism deletion/counterfactual composition of demographic vital rates is already an empirical modeling practice.

**Difference:** it is a species-specific matrix model, not a reusable necessary-and-sufficient double-dormancy theorem.

## Buddh, Krishna & Agashe (2024)

*Density dependent survival drives variation in density dependent population growth of an insect pest*. Oikos 2024(12):e10813. DOI **10.1111/oik.10813**.

The authors explicitly model density-dependent population growth as the **product** of density dependence in fecundity and survival and estimate the two component functions experimentally.

**Threat:** multiplicative survival–fecundity decomposition is not new as an ecological modeling architecture.

**Difference:** the paper does not formulate the frozen Double-Allee threshold-composition theorem.

## Pavlová, Berec & Boukal (2010)

*Caught between two Allee effects: Trade-off between reproduction and predation risk*. Journal of Theoretical Biology 264:787–798. DOI **10.1016/j.jtbi.2010.03.009**.

This paper models simultaneous mate-finding and predation Allee effects and analytically distinguishes survival/extinction scenarios generated by the interaction/trade-off.

**Threat:** two-component Allee interaction and mechanism-specific comparative effects are direct prior art.

**Difference:** model/trade-off specific, not a general component-function composition theorem.

# 3. Double dormancy exactness

Berec–Angulo–Courchamp (2007) remains decisive. Their Box 1:
- writes explicit reproduction and survival component effects;
- lets components be switched off;
- derives stability of extinction;
- shows that, in a key regime, zero or two positive equilibria may occur;
- states that no analytical expression was available to separate those two parameter regions, so they used numerical exploration.

The same paper identifies interaction among multiple Allee effects as an unresolved question.

Lan (2025), DOI **10.1016/j.chaos.2025.116952**, is the strongest recent direct threat: it rigorously studies deterministic and stochastic threshold dynamics in a single-species model with **two component Allee effects**.

# 4. Structural non-identifiability: the general idea is established

## Yates, Evans & Chappell (2009)

*Structural identifiability analysis via symmetries of differential equations*. Automatica 45(11):2585–2591. DOI **10.1016/j.automatica.2009.07.009**.

They construct Lie symmetries of coupled differential equations and identify the subalgebra that preserves the observation. Those transformations preserve the model/output structure and are explicitly linked to structural identifiability.

This directly subsumes the logic:

[
(A_1,ldots,A_m)
mapsto
(A_1h_1,ldots,A_mh_m),
qquad
prod_i h_i=1,
]

whenever the output/vector field depends only on (prod_i A_i).

The proposed factor gauge is therefore a particularly transparent infinite-dimensional/output-preserving symmetry, not a new abstract mechanism of structural non-identifiability.

## Massonis & Villaverde (2020)

*Finding and Breaking Lie Symmetries: Implications for Structural Identifiability and Observability in Biological Modelling*. Symmetry 12:469. DOI **10.3390/sym12030469**.

This work systematizes finding symmetries that cause structural unidentifiability/unobservability and transformations/reparameterizations that break them.

## Identifiable combinations literature

Meshkat, Anderson & DiStefano (2011), DOI **10.1016/j.mbs.2011.06.001**, proves theoretical results for identifiable parameter combinations and rational reparameterizations of nonlinear ODE input–output equations.

Eisenberg & Hayashi (2014), DOI **10.1016/j.mbs.2014.08.008**, explicitly frames identifiable combinations as functions of individually unidentifiable parameters and notes that measuring additional parameters can restore identifiability.

Villaverde & Banga (2017), DOI **10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005878**, shows that a biological invariance yielding unchanged outputs for different parameter values is structural unidentifiability under the corresponding observation scheme.

### Nonparametric distinction

The frozen (A_i(x)) are functions, not finite-dimensional parameters. No source was found that states the exact ecological nonparametric factorization as an ((m-1))-function gauge.

However, mathematically:

[
P=prod_i A_i
]

contains only one composite function. Without restrictions on the (A_i), factorization is nonunique, and the proposed (h_i) transformation explicitly exhibits that nonuniqueness.

This is a direct functional analogue of standard output-preserving symmetry/identifiable-combination theory.

**Classification of Candidate Theorem D:** **COVERED AFTER REFORMULATION.**

It is a useful ecological impossibility corollary, not a defensible standalone novelty claim.

# 5. Minimal observation design is established as a general problem

## Joubert, Stigter & Molenaar (2018)

*Determining minimal output sets that ensure structural identifiability*. PLOS ONE 13:e0207334. DOI **10.1371/journal.pone.0207334**.

The paper explicitly asks which outputs must be measured so that unique model parameters can be inferred and gives an algorithm for finding minimal output sets ensuring structural identifiability.

This directly blocks a generic claim that “minimal outputs restoring identifiability” is a new mathematical topic.

## Integrated population models

Riecke et al. (2019), *Integrated population models: Model assumptions and inference*. Methods in Ecology and Evolution. DOI **10.1111/2041-210X.13195**.

Integrated population models combine abundance/count and demographic data streams to estimate survival, reproduction and other demographic rates jointly; the review emphasizes that integrated data can supply information unavailable from single data sources and that inference depends critically on the observation/process model.

This is the ecological counterpart of the proposed “measure component-specific outputs” logic.

### Frozen m-factor case

If (P=prod_{i=1}^m A_i) is known and (m-1) unrestricted factor functions are measured, the last factor is algebraically recovered. Conversely, with unrestricted factors, fewer than (m-1) independent functional constraints generically leave functional gauge freedom.

That statement is valuable for experimental design but is close to dimension/counting plus the already-standard identifiability-symmetry framework.

**Classification of Candidate Theorem E:** **COVERED AFTER REFORMULATION.**

A publishable contribution would need a sharper ecological observation model—e.g. survival and breeding probabilities measured through realistic sampling operators, censoring, latent states, or partial state ranges—where the minimality result is not algebraically immediate.

# 6. Network and fractional invariance

## Network

If the known coupling term does not depend on the hidden factor labels and each local composite product (P_i) is unchanged, the complete network vector field is unchanged.

That is immediate once the local symmetry is recognized.

General network structural-identifiability theory already exists, including Varalda & Pequito (2026), DOI **10.1016/j.ifacsc.2026.100397**.

The 2026 strong-Allee N-patch paper by Ya-Lun Tsai, DOI **10.1016/j.jsc.2025.102544**, further confirms that generic network-Allee equilibrium/bifurcation structure is already active prior art.

**Classification:** **COVERED AFTER REFORMULATION**; retain only as a corollary.

# 7. Strongest result capable of killing each frozen subclaim

## Threshold composition

No single source kills the exact component-deletion function-class theorem. The strongest combination is:
- Berec et al. 2007 for double dormancy and explicit component switching;
- Lan 2025 for rigorous two-component threshold dynamics;
- Rodriguez 1988 / Feldman–Morris 2011 / Buddh et al. 2024 for multi-vital-rate/product demographic composition.

## Factorization non-identifiability

**Yates–Evans–Chappell (2009)** is the strongest conceptual killer.

Precise paraphrase of the relevant result:

> observation-preserving Lie symmetries generate transformations of a dynamical model that leave its observed behavior unchanged; these transformations are equivalent to the similarity-transformation approach used to diagnose structural identifiability.

The frozen product gauge is exactly such an output/vector-field-preserving transformation.

## Minimal observation

**Joubert–Stigter–Molenaar (2018)** is the strongest generic killer:

> structural identifiability can be posed directly as the problem of finding minimal sets of model outputs whose measurement guarantees unique parameter recovery, and the paper provides an iterative procedure to identify such sets.

The frozen proposal must therefore produce an ecology-specific theorem, not claim the general problem.

# 8. Final verdict on the integrated program

## What survives

A defensible research program may still exist around:

> **mechanism-resolved composition of separately measurable demographic component functions, with explicit component-deletion counterfactuals, where the mathematics derives rather than assumes conditions for emergent strong thresholds.**

The inverse section can then show a sharp scientific consequence:

> aggregate abundance may reveal the demographic threshold while being insufficient to attribute it uniquely to component mechanisms.

That second statement is important scientifically even though its structural-identifiability machinery is standard.

## What does not survive as headline novelty

Do **not** claim novelty for:
- unimodal two-crossing classification;
- saddle-node equations (V=1,V'=0);
- implicit-function comparative statics formula;
- output-preserving symmetry as a concept;
- factor-product non-identifiability in the abstract;
- generic minimal-output structural identifiability;
- fixed-operator/network preservation of an exactly unchanged RHS.

## Quality risk

The frozen framework is **not yet automatically Q1-level mathematics** merely because no identical paper was found.

If Theorem B reduces to:
1. assume the joint product is unimodal;
2. check its value at zero and its maximum;
3. invoke standard root counting,

while Theorems D/E are standard identifiability corollaries, the mathematical novelty is too thin.

The forward program becomes materially stronger if it proves, for a broad biologically meaningful component class:
- verifiable component-level conditions implying the required product shape;
- necessary and sufficient interaction conditions, not merely endpoint tests;
- a nontrivial classification of minimal threshold-generating subsets for (m) mechanisms;
- monotonicity or lattice/submodularity properties of threshold-generating subsets;
- robustness of those classifications under uncertainty;
- results that recover Berec-type, survival–fecundity, and other models as genuine corollaries.

## FINAL RETURN POSITION

No result resolving the **exact mechanism-resolved component-deletion composition theorem** was identified in the searched corpus as of 2026-09-28.

However, the proposed inverse-identifiability and minimal-output pieces are **not independent mathematical novelties at the general level stated**. They are applications/corollaries of established structural-identifiability theory.

Thus the integrated program is **not killed**, but it must be promoted only with the forward composition theorem as the mathematical novelty core and with a stronger theorem target than the current unimodality lemma.
