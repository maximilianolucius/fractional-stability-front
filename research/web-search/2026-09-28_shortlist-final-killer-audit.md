## 2. Family A — final killer logic

### Stable-set/hereditary search

Delay and hereditary systems have rich basin theory on history spaces.

Huang–Yang–Yi–Zou 2014 is especially relevant because:
- it is bistable;
- it has two stable equilibria;
- it studies basin subsets;
- it applies to an Allee population equation.

This demonstrates that hereditary basin geometry is mature as a topic.

But no theorem was found that makes the endpoint projection dynamically sufficient.

The specific implication

\[
\phi(0)=\psi(0)
\Longrightarrow
\omega(\phi)=\omega(\psi)
\]

is not generic hereditary-system theory.

### Positive/monotone search

Wu 2023 gives general system comparison principles for Caputo FDEs.

These imply strong restrictions for:
- quasi-monotone systems;
- mixed monotone systems;
- ordered initial states.

Combined with scalar/triangular nonintersection, the admissible theorem should distinguish:
- classes where multibasin fibers are impossible;
- classes where they remain possible.

The comparison theory does not establish endpoint-fiber basin uniqueness for arbitrary incomparable states.

### Natural model class

Mondal et al. 2025, DOI 10.1016/j.cjph.2025.09.020, supplies:
- fractional predator–prey;
- Double Allee;
- positive dynamics;
- stable extinction;
- multistability;
- coexistence;
- basin diagrams;
- incommensurate memory.

This is sufficient to show a natural application class exists.

It does not solve the fiber question.

### Final A residual

A mathematically clean program can ask:

1. when are present-state fibers basin-pure?
2. when are they multibasin?
3. what sign/monotonicity structures force purity?
4. what dimension is required?
5. how does the answer depend on \(\alpha\)?
6. what does this imply for extinction inference from current population observations?

## 3. Family B — final killer logic

### Robust persistence is already abstract

Smith–Zhao 2001 works with semidynamical systems.

Salceanu 2022 works with structured delay equations and robust uniform persistence.

Thus moving from finite-dimensional ODE to a history state is not enough for novelty.

### Fractional-index perturbations are continuous

Modern Caputo dynamics results include continuous dependence on the fractional index.

Therefore a future project should not premise its novelty on the claim that varying \(\alpha\) is outside perturbation theory.

### Where standard persistence does not fit strong Allee

For strong Allee:

\[
X_+
=
\mathcal B_{\rm extinction}
\cup
\Sigma
\cup
\mathcal B_{\rm survival}.
\]

Uniform permanence on all positive states is false.

The robust object is:
- persistence of this multibasin decomposition;
- survival/extinction inner sets common to an uncertainty family;
- movement or enclosure of \(\Sigma\);
- survival-side conditional persistence.

### Strong invariance

Ordinary inclusion theory already distinguishes:
- weak viability;
- strong invariance;
- discriminating kernels;
- capture basins.

Direct Caputo literature found in the audit is still dominated by viability/existence of selected trajectories.

No direct all-selections theorem was found that also handles uncertain order and the strong-Allee multibasin structure.

### Exactness problem

A generic common Lyapunov sublevel set is insufficiently distinctive.

The future mathematical question should be structural:
- maximal/common robust basin kernels;
- sharp separator bounds;
- topology-preservation conditions;
- loss of bistability under uncertainty.

### Final B residual

\[
\text{robust multibasin geometry}
\quad\text{under}\quad
(\theta,\alpha,\text{kernel/mechanism})\text{ perturbations}.
\]

## 4. Family C — final killer logic

### Földes–Stacy theorem-level compatibility

Their framework assumes:
- Polish Markov state;
- invariant extinction and persistence sets;
- Feller property;
- dissipative Lyapunov functions;
- average-Lyapunov negativity over invariant boundary measures;
- martingale controls;
- infinite-dimensional tightness.

Hamaguchi/Bianchi stochastic-Volterra lifts provide several of these ingredients:
- Hilbert state;
- Markov property;
- Feller-type structure;
- invariant-measure machinery.

Therefore abstract stochastic persistence transfer is plausible and should be viewed as technically nontrivial specialization, not a wholly new foundation.

### Strong-Allee distinction

Strong-Allee extinction may originate from an interior deterministic basin rather than only the coordinate boundary.

Hence standard coexistence-away-from-boundary persistence does not directly encode the biological question.

Still, this distinction is better treated as part of basin-exit geometry.

### Exit theory

Lipshutz 2018 proves for SDDEs:
- uniform path LDP over bounded initial histories;
- quasipotential on history space;
- small-noise exit-time asymptotics.

Thus infinite-dimensional/history-space exit theory itself is prior.

Jacquier–Pannier and Gao et al. provide SVE/power-law LDPs.

No direct source was found performing the full downstream exit construction for a singular fractional Volterra basin.

### Persistent switching

Herrera-Marín 2026 and its June follow-up already provide a serious persistent long-memory switching framework.

Switching is therefore demoted from novelty core to application architecture.

### Positive noise

Recent SVE invariance theory permits positive-cone-preserving multiplicative/square-root noise classes.

Ecological realism is feasible.

### Final C residual

The clean residual is:

\[
\text{uniform-in-initial-memory small-noise exit theory}
\]

for singular power-law stochastic Volterra/Caputo systems with positive multistable extinction/survival basins, including:
- quasipotential;
- most probable exit path;
- mean exit-time scaling;
- dependence on \(\alpha\).

## 5. Comparative interpretation

Family A has the cleanest novelty boundary because its exact statement is not a generic theorem imported from another discipline.

Family B overlaps heavily with robust persistence/viability and must move to the topology/geometry of multiple basins under memory-order perturbation.

Family C overlaps heavily with stochastic persistence, delay exit theory and stochastic-Volterra LDPs and must move to the singular-memory implementation of quasipotential/exit geometry.

This is an evidentiary comparison, not a final project recommendation.
