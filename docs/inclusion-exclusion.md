# Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

## Include when a work contributes

- a theorem/proposition on fractional stability;
- a materially sharper criterion or proof technique;
- a counterexample or sharpness result;
- structured-matrix mathematics relevant to fractional stability;
- topology-aware network stability;
- an explicit frontier/open problem;
- foundational adjacent mathematics needed to generalize the field;
- a high-quality review useful for terminology or corpus expansion;
- a mathematically substantive Allee/Double Allee result.

## Retain as application-level when

The paper mainly applies existing criteria to a specific biological, engineering, economic, neural, or epidemiological model.

These can still reveal repeated assumptions, common misuse, empirical structure, or open application-driven questions.

## Exclude from theorem synthesis when

- purely numerical with no reusable analytical result;
- unrelated meaning of “stability”;
- substantive duplicate;
- source cannot be verified enough to support the claim.

## Controlled exclusion reasons

- OUT_OF_SCOPE
- APPLICATION_ONLY
- DUPLICATE
- INSUFFICIENT_MATHEMATICAL_CONTENT
- SOURCE_UNVERIFIED
- DIFFERENT_STABILITY_CONCEPT
- SUPERSEDED
