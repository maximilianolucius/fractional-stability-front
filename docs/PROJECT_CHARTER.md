# Project Charter — Fractional Stability Front

**Authoritative mission as of 2026-09-28**

## Domain

The domain to be mapped is:

> **Dynamical Systems and Stability Theory — Fractional Differential Equations**

The project covers the mathematical theory of dynamical systems governed by fractional differential equations, with emphasis on stability, spectral geometry, robustness, bifurcation, persistence/extinction, structured systems, networks, uncertainty, and closely related dynamical phenomena.

## Primary objective

Construct a rigorous, theorem-level map of the current state of knowledge and use that map to identify **fertile research gaps**.

A fertile gap is not merely a topic with few papers. It should plausibly support future original mathematics of sufficient depth and generality for a strong Q1-level research paper.

The project does not attempt to solve that gap.

## Double Allee requirement

Among the identified opportunities, preference is given to a gap that:

1. directly involves a **Double Allee / Multiple Allee model**, or
2. if that is not mathematically strong enough, lies **very close** to Double/Multiple Allee dynamics in mechanism, threshold geometry, persistence/extinction, bifurcation, robustness, network structure, or fractional stability.

The ecological connection must be mathematically natural, not decorative.

The endpoint is an evidence-backed answer to:

> **Where, within or very near Double Allee dynamics, is the most fertile unresolved mathematical territory revealed by the state of the art in fractional dynamical systems and stability theory?**

## Explicit non-objectives

This project does **not**:

- complete proofs for a newly proposed theorem program;
- develop the selected gap into a finished original-research contribution;
- write the eventual research paper that solves the gap;
- optimize a particular candidate merely because early searches look promising.

Those tasks belong to a separate future project.

## Legitimate final products

The expected outputs are:

1. a structured state-of-the-art corpus;
2. a theorem/result map;
3. a frontier map;
4. an open-problem catalogue;
5. a bridge map across adjacent mathematical literatures;
6. a portfolio of candidate research gaps;
7. adversarial novelty audits for the strongest candidates;
8. a reasoned recommendation of fertile directions, with Double Allee proximity explicit;
9. optionally, a **survey / review / state-of-the-art paper** based on the completed knowledge map.

## Unit of analysis

The unit of analysis is the **mathematical result**, not the paper.

For each result record:

- system class;
- fractional operator/order architecture;
- stability/dynamical notion;
- assumptions;
- exact theorem strength;
- proof technique;
- dimension/topology restrictions;
- robustness;
- relation to prior results;
- unresolved extensions;
- relevance to Double Allee or neighboring threshold dynamics.

## Opportunity standard

A candidate gap should be evaluated by:

- evidence that it is genuinely unresolved;
- mathematical depth;
- generality/unification potential;
- exactness potential;
- robustness significance;
- closeness to the fractional-dynamical-systems core;
- closeness to Double/Multiple Allee;
- plausible empirical/applied interpretation;
- prior-art density;
- risk of being only a cosmetic model variation.

No candidate is promoted merely because its exact phrase is absent from search results.

## Survey option

Once the map is mature, the project may pivot its writing effort toward a survey such as:

> **Dynamical Systems and Stability Theory for Fractional Differential Equations: A Theorem-Level Map of the Frontier**

with a dedicated section on threshold/persistence phenomena and research opportunities near Double Allee dynamics.
