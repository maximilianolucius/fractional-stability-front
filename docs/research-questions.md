# Research Questions

## RQ1 — Domain architecture
What are the major theorem families in **Dynamical Systems and Stability Theory — Fractional Differential Equations**, and how should they be partitioned without conflating non-equivalent notions?

## RQ2 — Foundational stability
What are the canonical exact spectral/characteristic-root criteria for commensurate and incommensurate fractional systems?

## RQ3 — Theorem strength
For every major branch, what is the strongest known result: exact, N&S, sufficient, converse, perturbative, computational, numerical, or impossibility?

## RQ4 — Nonlinear frontier
What local/global nonlinear stability results exist, and where do linearization, Lyapunov, Mittag-Leffler, comparison, invariant-set and converse theories stop?

## RQ5 — Operator/order frontier
How do results change across Caputo, Riemann–Liouville, multi-term, variable-order, distributed-order, uncertain-order, commensurate and incommensurate formulations?

## RQ6 — Structured systems
How do fractional stability results interact with D-stability, diagonal stability, Metzler/positive systems, M-matrices, sign/qualitative matrices and structured uncertainty?

## RQ7 — Robustness
Which deterministic results remain valid under parameter uncertainty, order uncertainty, perturbation, delay, switching, stochastic forcing, impulses, or time variation?

## RQ8 — Networks
Which results genuinely depend on graph topology, directed/signed structure, multilayer coupling, switching topology, synchronization/consensus, or dispersal?

## RQ9 — Bifurcation and thresholds
What is known about saddle-node, Hopf-type fractional bifurcation, multistability, tipping, persistence/extinction and resilience in fractional systems?

## RQ10 — Exactness/converse gaps
Where does the literature rely mainly on sufficient LMIs/certificates despite a missing exact characterization or converse?

## RQ11 — Dimension/structure gaps
Which results remain restricted to low dimension, special sign patterns, matrix classes, rational order ratios, or special graph topologies?

## RQ12 — Bridge gaps
Which mature adjacent literatures contain mathematics that has not been fully imported into fractional dynamical systems?

## RQ13 — Double Allee state of art
What is known mathematically about Double/Multiple Allee mechanisms, including component/demographic effects, double dormancy, multiple thresholds, dispersal, networks, delay, stochasticity and fractional formulations?

## RQ14 — Double Allee adjacency
Which unresolved fractional-stability or threshold problems are directly Double Allee, and which are very close enough that Double Allee naturally exposes the missing mathematics?

## RQ15 — Opportunity quality
Which candidate gaps have enough mathematical depth, generality and novelty to motivate a future Q1-level original-research project?

## RQ16 — Candidate falsification
For each strong candidate, what is the strongest prior result that could make it redundant or mathematically trivial?

## RQ17 — Data relevance
For near-Double-Allee opportunities, are there real datasets capable of observing the relevant mechanisms/thresholds without forcing an artificial empirical component?

## RQ18 — Survey readiness
Is the theorem map mature and coherent enough to support a survey/state-of-the-art paper, and what would its organizing mathematical narrative be?
