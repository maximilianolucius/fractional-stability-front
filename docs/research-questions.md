# Research Questions

## RQ1 — Landscape
What system classes, fractional operators, stability notions, matrix structures, and network structures have been studied?

## RQ2 — Strength
For each major subproblem, what is the strongest known result: exact, necessary, sufficient, perturbative, computational, numerical, or impossibility?

## RQ3 — Dimensional frontier
Which results remain restricted to fixed or low dimension, and what blocks extension to arbitrary dimension?

## RQ4 — Operator/order frontier
How do results change under commensurate/incommensurate, Caputo/Riemann–Liouville, variable-order, distributed-order, or uncertain-order formulations?

## RQ5 — Structured matrices
How do fractional stability results interact with D-stability, diagonal stability, positive/Metzler systems, M-matrices, sign stability, qualitative matrices, interval matrices, and structured uncertainty?

## RQ6 — Networks
Which results genuinely exploit graph topology, sparsity, switching, signed interactions, multilayer structure, or temporal networks?

## RQ7 — Conservatism
Where are Lyapunov/LMI criteria conservative relative to spectral or exact characterizations?

## RQ8 — Robustness
Which exact deterministic results survive perturbations, uncertainty, delay, switching, stochastic forcing, or time-varying topology?

## RQ9 — Bridge gaps
Which mature mathematical literatures contain tools relevant to fractional stability but are weakly connected to it?

## RQ10 — Open problems
Which open problems are explicit, theorem-implied, cross-literature, or discovered only after systematic comparison?

## RQ11 — Unification
Which missing theorem would make the largest family of current results special cases or corollaries?

## RQ12 — Double Allee
Can a Double Allee mechanism expose a new structural stability, bifurcation, robustness, topology, or identifiability problem of independent mathematical interest?

## RQ13 — Data
Are there real datasets rich enough to distinguish competing Double Allee mechanisms and, where relevant, identify fractional memory or network effects?

## RQ14 — Research program
What dependency structure connects the strongest open problems, and which one should be attacked first?
